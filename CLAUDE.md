# Formulix

## Commits

- Subject only, in English, with no gitmoji and no type prefix (`feat:`, `fix:`).
- Capitalized, no trailing period, 4 to 10 words.
- Describes the resulting state, not the action: "Driver stats recomputed from race results", not "Add driver stats recomputation".
- Neither the French commits of autumn 2026 nor the older gitmoji ones are the reference.
